FAQ

Common questions

Do you work in our existing org?

Yes. I start with a review of what's already there, then build in a sandbox of your org so nothing changes in production until you approve it.

Can you work with health data?

Yes. I've built HIPAA-aware flows for a telehealth clinic. Health data only moves once the right agreements are signed and access is limited to the people who need it.

What if we don't have the right system yet?

No problem. I work across Salesforce, GoHighLevel, QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho Books, NetSuite, Zapier, custom-built websites and many other platforms, so I can recommend what fits your team and budget. I've also led full system moves, including setup, data migration, team training and go-live. We can scope that as its own project.

How do you price?

Pricing depends on the scope of tasks, the workload and the turnaround time you need. I offer fixed-price projects, hourly work and monthly retainers. Tell me what you need and when, and I'll send a clear quote before any work starts.

What happens after launch?

You get written docs and a support window. Ongoing admin and support is available as a monthly block of hours.
